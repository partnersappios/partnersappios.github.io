Partners Privacy Policy

Partners is an app for keeping notes about the people close to you. This policy explains what happens to what you put in it.

What Partners collects: nothing. Partners has no account, no sign-in and no server. It doesn't send your notes, people, dates, places, photos or anything else to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.

Where your data lives: on your iPhone, inside the app, protected by iOS's data protection. Photos you add stay on your phone.

Widgets: if you add a Partners widget, the app keeps a small summary for it on your phone, which the widget reads. By default it holds kinds, days, times and counts only; initials, or names and titles, only if you choose that in Settings → Widgets. It never holds your notes, questions or the titles of threads. It's protected once you've unlocked your phone after it starts, so a widget can show it, and it's deleted when you remove your last widget. On the Lock Screen, anything beyond kinds, times and counts stays hidden until Face ID has seen you.

When your data leaves your phone, it's because you sent it, through iOS:

  • Calendar: if you link a calendar in Settings, the dates and events you choose to add go to that calendar, at the detail level you pick. That calendar's account (for example Google or iCloud) then holds them under its own terms.
  • iCloud Backup: if iCloud Backup is on for your iPhone, it includes Partners' data, as it does for other apps, unless you turn off Include in iPhone backup in Partners' Settings → Data. Apple holds the backup under its terms; with Advanced Data Protection on, it is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Exports: an export you make goes only where you send it. The encrypted export needs your passphrase to open. The readable export isn't encrypted, and Partners warns you before making one.
  • Siri: to recognize "Note about <name> in Partners", the app registers the names of the people you add, and any other names you give them, with iOS on your phone.
  • On-device suggestions use Apple's model on your phone. Your notes are never sent to an AI service.

TestFlight: while Partners is in testing, Apple's TestFlight may send crash reports to the developer if you allow it in your iPhone's settings. Feedback and screenshots reach the developer only if you choose to send them. Please don't include real people's details in them.

Deleting your data: deleting a person for good in Partners removes them and everything that is only theirs. Deleting the app deletes its data from your phone. Copies you made (exports and iCloud backups) are yours to delete where they are. Deleting a person for good can also remove the calendar entries Partners added for them; otherwise those stay in that calendar.

Children: Partners isn't directed at children.
